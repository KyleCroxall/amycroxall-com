People are at the heart of everything I do

I've spent over two decades working with people — leading teams, building cultures, and helping organisations bring out the best in their workforce. From managing busy retail operations and leading night teams at Tesco to stepping into bakery management, every role has reinforced my belief that investing in people is always the smartest strategy.
